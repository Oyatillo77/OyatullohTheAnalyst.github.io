Who Earns Over $50K? Income Analysis with SQL

Which factors are linked to earning over $50K a year? I cleaned a 48K-row census dataset in MySQL (checked duplicates, handled missing values), then compared high-income rates across age, workclass, and marital status using CTEs and window functions. The biggest surprise: Private-sector workers are the largest group, yet only 21.8% of them earn over $50K, compared to 55.4% of incorporated self-employed workers.

My First Excel Project: Tour Sales Dashboard

I analyzed 4,758 customers and 898 purchases (18.9% purchase rate) to find who buys tour packages. Key insight: The more follow-ups, the more sales: 12% after 1 contact vs 40% after 6. Young (18–30), single customers with a passport are the most likely to buy. Recommendation: Follow up 4–5 times and target young customers with a passport.
